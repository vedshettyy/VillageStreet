Morning on Bank Street

Walk the lane, enter the bank, and step up to the glowing models on the plaza to inspect them.

Click to start (on Quest, press Enter VR instead)

Press E (or VR trigger) to hold the object
Controls
Desktop: WASD / arrows walk · Shift faster · mouse look (or drag) · scroll zoom
F first/third person · E hold object · M sound
Kiosk: aim the dot at its screen, click or Enter (VR: point + trigger)
VR: left stick glide · right stick snap-turn 30° · A/X sound · trigger holds object
